Welcome to Shark Club!

We are a student-led community dedicated to shark science, ocean literacy, and conservation. We bring curious people together to learn from researchers, build field skills, and advocate for healthy oceans.

Our events are open to students from every background. No marine biology experience—and no scuba certification—is required.

WHAT WE DO

Benefits of participating

Learn alongside working scientists, practice responsible field observation, and meet people who care about sharks as much as you do.

  • Research talks and career conversations with shark scientists
  • Coastal field days, public outreach, and citizen-science projects
  • Workshops in identification, data skills, science communication, and conservation
